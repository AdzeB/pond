One place for Sources
and their downloads.

How can Pond show where Saves come from, what is downloading, and what needs attention without sending people between two pages?

Recommended direction: a shared Sources workspace

Keep Sources as one navigation destination. Within it, show All Downloads plus a compact list of connected Accounts and Watches. Selecting a source filters the download table; selecting a download reveals its details. Connection problems and retry actions stay in that same context.

How the references fit: use 04 for the shared frame, 02 for source controls, 01 for status summaries, 03 for optional expansion, and 05 for the compact Status feed.

Tradeoff: a Source rail costs horizontal space but keeps origin and recovery visible. Collapse the inspector at narrow widths. Keep quiet Sources accessible, distinguish Account/Watch from platform, and do not bury manual downloads. Source-level pause and per-Save cancel must remain distinct.
