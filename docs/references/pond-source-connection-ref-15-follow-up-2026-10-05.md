## Design question

What other connection experiences share reference 15's calm, product-like
handoff: paired identity, generous negative space, one plain-language activity
line, and one quiet progress cue?

Twenty additional elements were visually inspected. Inspection was limited to
visible previews and video posters. “Uploaded by” records the visible Cosmos
uploader and may not identify the original designer. Original-source links and
reuse terms were unavailable. No assets or code were copied.

## Direction

Reference 15 suggests a stronger alternative to a conventional spinner: show the
relationship itself. Pair the source mark with a small Pond or destination mark,
name the active handoff below it, and put one slim progress cue under the message.
Keep the dialog otherwise empty. Resolve success and failure in the same geometry.

The strongest additional references are 1, 3, 4, 7, and 13. Together they
support paired service identity, a literal handoff path, concise trust context,
and spatial continuity through completion.
