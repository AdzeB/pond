Why this one works

Two identities overlap to make the relationship concrete. The active message sits directly beneath them. A single narrow progress bar carries the motion while the rest of the frame stays almost empty.

Recommended refinement for Pond

Keep the X tile, but pair it with a small Pond mark or destination glyph so the task reads as a handoff. Replace the full orbit with a slim progress line below the paired identities. Keep the current success and failure continuity, but let the two tiles gently draw together while waiting.
