A connection should feel alive, not merely busy.

How can Pond turn a small source-connection dialog into a calm, trustworthy transition that keeps the service identity visible?

Recommended direction: one orbital connection object

Keep the source tile as the stable center. Move progress into a generous ring around it, let one moving marker signal ongoing work, and resolve that same geometry into a complete green or red ring. This preserves identity while making the state legible without adding another card or technical step list.

Stable centerThe source logo never moves or changes shape.
Continuous stateWaiting, success, and failure share one silhouette.
Quiet motionOne orbit plus a slow halo; both stop for reduced motion.
