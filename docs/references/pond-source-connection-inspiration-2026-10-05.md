# Pond source-connection visual research

Date: 5 October 2026  
Source: [Cosmos search results](https://www.cosmos.so/search/elements/connection%20loading%20UI)  

## Design question

How should Pond show a source connection in progress so the state feels calm,
continuous, and trustworthy while keeping the service identity visible?

Twenty distinct results were visually inspected from the Cosmos Elements search.
Inspection was limited to visible previews and video posters. “Uploaded by” records
the visible Cosmos uploader and may not identify the original designer. Original
source links and reuse terms were unavailable. No assets or code were copied.

## Direction

Use the source tile as a stable center inside a generous progress ring. A single
moving segment and marker communicate waiting. When the connection resolves, the
same ring fills with the success or failure color and the marker changes to a state
glyph. A quiet halo adds depth without introducing a new illustration.

The strongest references are 1, 2, 7, 10, and 13. Together they support stable
geometry across states, one strong motion signal, a larger central progress object,
and identity tied directly to progress.

The implementation is an adaptation of those general patterns, not a copy of any
individual reference. The full set, observations, and application notes are in the
[visual board](source-connection-visual-research-2026-10-05/index.html).
