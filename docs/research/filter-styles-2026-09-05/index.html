Live capture · 05 September 2026 · 19 Eagle filters inventoried

Every filter style.
Eagle beside Pond.

Pond already has specialized menus. The largest remaining visual gap is the repeated operator list above the actual controls. Keep Pond’s explicit semantics; make the active choice compact and let each editor fit its task.

01 / Collapse operator rows

One named selector, instead of two to four permanent rows. Applies across nearly every family.

02 / Fix range editors

Inline bounds and units. Combine dimensions; clarify size units; reveal advanced choices only when needed.

03 / Fill specific gaps

Color swatches, exact extensions, optional date buckets and arbitrary ratios. BPM needs metadata support.

17 Eagle menus captured; Font and Camera unavailable visually. Screenshots are untouched, shown through tight viewports at their captured scale; click any image for the complete window. App zoom/window sizes differ, so these are visual comparisons, not measured CSS dimensions. Results, selected states, custom calendars, AI quality, and full accessibility were not tested. Suggested changes are design judgments, not claims of Eagle’s hidden behavior.
