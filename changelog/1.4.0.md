## Important

- Video downloads are now off by default. Turn on Download Videos Automatically in Settings, or download any video from its menu.

## New

- Copy, paste, align, lock, and reorder items on a Canvas, with a right-click menu for all of it.
- Add sections, tables, code blocks, mind maps, and more shapes to a Canvas.
- Export a Canvas as PNG or PDF.
- Open a source to see its saves, storage, schedule, and fetch history on one page.
- Set download quality for all sources, or override it per source.

## Improved

- Harvesting settings are split into Schedule, Sources, Images, and Videos.
- Removing a video from a save keeps it removed after you restart Pond.

## Fixed

- Natural-language search works in installed builds on every platform.
- Windows antivirus no longer flags Pond as a threat.
